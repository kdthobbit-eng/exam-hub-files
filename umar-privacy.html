Privacy Policy — Umar Calculator: Age & Exam

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Developer: Kivo Labs (Exam Hub)

Umar Calculator works fully offline on your device. It calculates age and exam age-eligibility from the dates you enter.

  • The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.
  • Dates you enter stay only on your device and are not sent anywhere. The app has no internet permission.
  • No account, no login, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
  • The "Share" button only opens Android's share sheet with text you choose to send.
  • The Telegram button opens a public link (t.me/examhub2026) in your browser/Telegram app.

This is not a government app and is not affiliated with any government body. Always verify age limits in the official recruitment notification.

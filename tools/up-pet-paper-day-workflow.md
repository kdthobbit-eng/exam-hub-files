Is file ko kisi bhi nayi chat me upload karo. Isme paper wale din ka pura tarika, post ke text, file naam aur quiz ke niyam hain, taaki har shift ka kaam ek jaisa ho.

---

## 2. Paper wale din ka kram (har shift ke liye)

1. **Paper PDF turant post karo.** Exam Hub group me message 1 ke saath bhejo. File nayi upload karo, forward nahi.
2. **Answer key jaldi hal karo** (lagbhag 10 minute).
   - DI ke chitra (graph/table) mat banao.
   - Saare DI prashn hal karo. Data jahan tak ho sake text me likho, warna sirf uttar aur calculation do.
   - Har uttar ke saath official source ka link do (NCERT, PIB, NIOS, IGNOU aadi).
3. **Message 2 aur 3 ek saath bhejo:**
   - Message 2: quiz wali picture aur bold caption (picture aur caption ek hi post me).
   - Message 3: answer key ki PDF.
4. **UP PET group me quiz bhejo.** Har prashn ke ant me shift ka tag lagao.

## 6. Telegram quiz (UP PET group) ke niyam

- Har prashn ke ant me tag: **(PET 01 Nov 2026 I Shift)**, doosri shift me **(PET 01 Nov 2026 II Shift)**
- Prashn adhiktam 300 akshar, har vikalp adhiktam 100 akshar.
- Paanch vikalp: A, B, C, D aur E = "उपर्युक्त में से कोई नहीं" (agar paper me ho).
- Sahi uttar ki vyakhya adhiktam 200 akshar ki ho.
- "Hard", "Tough", "Easy" jaise shabd kabhi mat likho.
- Quiz poll prakar ka ho; sahi uttar aur vyakhya attempt ke baad dikhe.

## Caption rule (3 Nov 2026)
- `send_file` caption markdown parse karta hai: poora caption `**...**` me likho, ek hi step me bold aata hai. `<b>` tag mat likho, wo raw dikh jata hai. Link chahiye to bhejne ke baad `edit_message` (parse_mode=html) se `<a href>` lagao.
- Kabhi bhi placeholder caption (jaise "platoon") se file mat bhejo, seedha final caption ke saath bhejo.
- Caption pura bold, chhota; brackets "(...)" aur extra heading mat lagao.

## Quiz wording rule (user, 4 Nov 2026)
Quiz ke sawal kathin (hard) banao, par channel message, quiz heading ya kahin bhi "hard / हार्ड / कठिन लेवल" mat likho. Message simple aur bold (HTML <b>) rakho.
